One image that sums up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026 as an illustrated front page: Russia's strike of 400 drones and 110 missiles on Ukraine, with two missiles down in Poland; the AI stock rout that pushed the S&P 500 into correction; the World Court holding Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya; the daraxonrasib pill that left pancreatic tumours undetectable in 1 in 12 trial patients; and Trump defending his ties to Putin and Xi after the Shenzhen trilateral, with a ticker of the day's other stories. The entry program (main.ts, Node 22, standard library only) copies the image to /output/entry.png; compose.py rebuilds the image from the panel illustrations in assets/.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
